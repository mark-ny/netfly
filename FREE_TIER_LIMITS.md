# Launch budget and provider terms

Reviewed on 25 November 2026. Figures below are estimates in USD before tax, in decimal GB. Provider dashboards and invoices are authoritative for the owner's account. This review used public documentation. It did not inspect an account, enter checkout or reserve capacity.

## What the catalog can fit

For each title, multiply duration in seconds by the sum of the selected video and audio bitrates in bits/s, divide by eight and add packaging, subtitles and artwork. This model allows 5% for HLS packaging and 2 MB per title for assets. Assume the whole catalog stays in R2 Standard for a full billing month. Originals, backups, alternate audio, temporary duplicate encodes and trailers require separate space.

| Catalog scenario | Encoded storage | Storage overage/month |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Eight 18-second studies at all three qualities | 0.093 GB | $0 |
| Twenty 90-minute films, 360p only | 9.91 GB | $0 at modeled size |
| Twenty 90-minute films, 480p only | 15.58 GB | $0.09 |
| Twenty 90-minute films, 720p only | 32.59 GB | about $0.35 |
| Twenty 90-minute films, all three qualities | 57.99 GB | $0.72 |

The ladder uses 600, 1,000 and 2,200 kb/s video, each with 96 kb/s audio. For example, the full ladder totals 4,088 kb/s. A 90-minute film uses approximately 2.90 GB. Two films use 5.80 GB and three use 8.70 GB. Twenty at 360p would leave about 94 MB of headroom. A 95% storage guardrail would stop that upload. A two-film full-ladder pilot leaves space for replacements. For all twenty features, obtain approval for overages and measure actual encode sizes first. R2 rounds usage to billing units. [R2 pricing and examples](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing/)

## Billing decisions and guardrails

Cloudflare requires R2 subscription checkout and a payment method for usage billing. Keep development on a local bucket until the owner authorizes activation. R2 can accrue overages automatically. The published account-wide budget alerts send email. Workers and Durable Objects offer separate spending limits for supported usage, subject to enforcement delay and exclusions. Neither those limits nor email alerts enforce a hard R2 invoice maximum. Supabase's Pro Spend Cap covers specific usage classes. Compute, custom domains, backups with point-in-time recovery and other explicitly selected add-ons can still incur costs. GitHub offers a stop-usage setting on supported product budgets. [R2 activation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/get-started/) · [Payment method requirements](https://developers.cloudflare.com/billing/troubleshoot/resolve-cannot-remove-payment-method/) · [Cloudflare budget alerts](https://developers.cloudflare.com/billing/manage/budget-alerts/) · [Workers spending limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/spend-management/) · [Supabase spend cap](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/cost-control) · [GitHub budgets](https://docs.github.com/en/billing/reference/product-usage-included)

Use 70%, 85% and 95% of the chosen budget as warning levels. At 70%, reconcile local estimates with provider usage including other projects. At 85%, hold higher-resolution additions and allow for an in-progress replacement. At 95%, pause ingestion and new playback grants as appropriate to the constrained resource. Use the remaining allowance for active sessions and recovery. Separate daily Workers thresholds from monthly storage and operations. A browser setting, local calculator or stale dashboard cannot enforce an account-wide bill ceiling.

## Rights and commercial operation

Review the owner's current order terms before launch. Cloudflare's self-serve CDN terms direct non-enterprise video delivery to eligible services such as its Developer Platform. The proposed path uses R2 as the origin with a Worker gateway. Supabase's service terms permit use subject to the plan, the customer's business purposes and use restrictions. GitHub Actions is for the repository's development lifecycle. These services supply infrastructure. Distribution rights for a specific film, soundtrack, trailer, poster and subtitles must come from their respective rightsholders. [Cloudflare CDN terms](https://www.cloudflare.com/service-specific-terms-application-services/) · [Cloudflare's R2 video guidance](https://blog.cloudflare.com/updated-tos) · [Supabase terms](https://supabase.com/terms) · [GitHub additional product terms](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-for-additional-products-and-features)

For a Kenyan public launch, have the operator and local counsel review distribution registration, title classifications, geographic and age restrictions, the legal notices and the takedown channel. Document the controller's identity, purpose and lawful basis for each data category, data subject access/correction/objection/deletion processes, actual retention periods, processor agreements and safeguards for any cross-border transfers. Assess ODPC registration requirements and exceptions for the business. Establish incident triage for the Act's 72-hour commissioner notification duty when the statutory real-risk-of-harm threshold is met. Consumer launch clearance and classification depend on the operator and films.
